Sector change is the equal-weighted average move of its Nifty 500 members (Industry column of the NSE list). Turnover = price × volume so far. “Vol vs 20d” is today's volume so far divided by the average full-day volume of the last 20 sessions, so it reads well under 1× early in the session — compare stocks with each other rather than against 1. R-factor (0–100, F&O stocks): each stock is ranked among all F&O stocks on three things - volume vs its 20-day average, open interest added since 09:15, and the size of the price move since 09:15 - and R is the geometric average of the three ranks, so it is high only when volume, OI and price move are all high together; it is 0 when OI has fallen. ▲ = OI up with price up (long buildup), ▼ = OI up with price down (short buildup) - both measured since 09:15, so the tag can differ from the Chg % column, which is against yesterday's close. It is a ranking aid for finding candidates, not a signal. Data comes from Upstox and is refreshed every couple of minutes; it is not a recommendation to trade.
