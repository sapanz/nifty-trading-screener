Highest volume

Sector change is the equal-weighted average move of its Nifty 500 members (Industry column of the NSE list). Turnover = price × volume so far. “Vol vs 20d” is today's volume so far divided by the average full-day volume of the last 20 sessions, so it reads well under 1× early in the session — compare stocks with each other rather than against 1. R-factor (0–100, F&O stocks): each stock is ranked among all F&O stocks on three things - volume vs its 20-day average, open interest added since 09:15, and the size of the price move since 09:15 - and R is the geometric average of the three ranks, so it is high only when volume, OI and price move are all high together; it is 0 when OI has fallen. ▲ = OI up with price up (long buildup), ▼ = OI up with price down (short buildup) - both measured since 09:15, so the tag can differ from the Chg % column, which is against yesterday's close. It is a ranking aid for finding candidates, not a signal. Swing (built each evening after the close) applies the same idea to daily charts: volume vs its 20-day average, OI added over the last 5 sessions (near + next month futures) and the 5-session price move; ⚠ roll marks a window that includes a futures expiry, when the OI change includes settled and rolled positions. Order block (5-min, F&O stocks; Live and Replay): in an up-move the last red candle before the thrust that breaks the recent swing high (mirror for shorts). Its range is the zone; price often comes back to it before the move resumes, and the zone's low (plus 0.1%) is a tight stop - the "stop" figure is how far that is from the live price. “Waiting” = the thrust happened and price hasn't come back yet; “retesting” = it touched the zone in the last 15 minutes without breaking it. Scanned every 5 minutes on completed candles, only for zones formed today. In the Swing tab the same idea runs on daily candles (zones formed in the last 40 sessions, rebuilt each evening). Whether this has an edge is checked by backtest - treat it as a way to find candidates and place a stop, not as a signal. Price action (built each evening after the close; Nifty 500 plus every F&O stock): weekly support zones and weekly breakouts - range, triangle, cup and handle - with a daily close as the trigger, a stop under the structure and the next weekly resistance as the target, sorted by reward : risk. The probability is measured on each night's own history and scaled to what its year-by-year test delivered. Data comes from Upstox and is refreshed every couple of minutes; it is not a recommendation to trade.
