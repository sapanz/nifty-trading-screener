Sector change is the equal-weighted average move of its Nifty 500 members (Industry column of the NSE list). Turnover = price × volume so far. “Vol vs 20d” is today's volume so far divided by the average full-day volume of the last 20 sessions, so it reads well under 1× early in the session — compare stocks with each other rather than against 1. Data comes from Upstox and is refreshed every couple of minutes; it is not a recommendation to trade.
